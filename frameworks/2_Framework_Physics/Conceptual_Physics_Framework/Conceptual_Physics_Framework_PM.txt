I-CAN-FIRST CURRICULUM SPINE — HARD
------------------------------------
The exact Assessment Plan I-can statement is the ATOMIC planning, evidence, and routing address for the Bank and downstream curriculum. Learning Targets group related I-cans; Mastery Goals organize/report accumulated evidence.

For Bank authoring, NEVER work in this order:
  section quota -> questions -> attach I-can labels.

Work in this order:
  section -> exact I-cans -> evidence specification/mini-bank for each I-can -> question families/representations/misconceptions/DOK -> downstream routes -> reconcile section totals.

Read `curriculum/i_can_index.json` and `contracts/i_can_first_bank_contract.json`. Every canonical item has exactly one PRIMARY `i_can_id`; synthesis items may additionally list `supporting_i_can_ids`. Unequal I-can totals are allowed when the evidence job warrants them, but zero coverage is forbidden. A large section bank does not compensate for a missing I-can pathway.

Coverage floors are HARD: each I-can gets a complete Notes Example/YTI pair; every Practice Set includes every section I-can; CYU and combined Warm-Ups include every section I-can; each parallel Exit A/B/C directly samples every section I-can; every Summative form V1-V6 directly samples every exact Unit I-can at least once.

QUESTION-DESIGN MAP — HARD
--------------------------
Read the dedicated `_question_structure.zip` and `contracts/question_design_map_contract.json` BEFORE final prompt authoring. The Physics sequence is:
  exact I-can -> evidence job -> content modes -> formula/reverse paths -> vocabulary directions -> representations/tools -> best-fit structures -> parallel variation axes -> JSON item slots -> author -> solve/verify -> derive DOK/Bloom/final family metadata.

Every fresh Physics Bank includes `unitN_question_design_map.json`. Variety is planned from the I-can, never imposed as a universal quota. The map may plan direct/inverse formula work, dependent multi-step chains, vocabulary/matching, graph/data, diagram/FBD/vector/model work, misconception diagnosis, short explanation, and transfer when they fit. Some I-cans should heavily favor quantitative/formula work; others should not.

For formula-capable I-cans, do not let generic conceptual/vocabulary questions crowd out the mathematical relationship students actually need to use. Vary which quantity is unknown and which givens are supplied when the relationship supports it. Multi-step means a dependent intermediate result; when that result exists only to reach one final answer, normally write one natural problem rather than artificial part (a)/(b). Keep units compatible unless conversion is itself the target.

`question_family_id` is semantic. Do not append V1/Q12/Exit-B style slot tokens merely to make parallel items look unique.

PHYSICS DESTINATION AUTHORING PREFERENCES
-----------------------------------------
• PHYSICAL SITUATIONS: do not author naked plug-and-chug prompts that merely assign variables and ask students to solve. Begin with a concrete physical situation/phenomenon that naturally supplies the measurements. Equations are relationships describing the phenomenon, not answer machines.
• EXAMPLE/YTI: when an I-can naturally supports formula/quantitative application, a strong contextual quantitative pair takes priority over generic conceptual or vocabulary filler. Example models; YTI applies with meaningfully changed evidence.
• PRACTICE: map the mix before authoring. Use matching, vocabulary, direct/inverse formula work, dependent multi-step, graphs/data, diagrams/FBDs/vectors, short conceptual response, misconception diagnosis, and transfer only where the exact I-can supports them. No fixed percentages.
• WARM-UPS: preserve the established retrieval/bridging pattern; controlled repetition is useful.
• EXIT TICKETS: plan variety within the four questions and across A/B/C. Do not default to four same-shell variants. Physics Exit Tickets are formative-secure by destination; a weaker metadata label cannot bypass semantic-independence QA.
• WTC: preserve the normal Bank card/metadata/Answer/Alignment format. The workbook/released FRQ role is morphology reference only.

PHYSICS WTC — FRQ BREAKDOWN ROUTINE — HARD
-------------------------------------------
Every Physics WTC is a true free-response-style multipart task, not a loose preview list. Use one shared physics situation, graph, table, diagram, data set, model, or phenomenon and normally 3-5 connected parts labeled (a), (b), (c)... . The purpose is repeated practice in BREAKING DOWN long questions: identify givens/unknowns, extract evidence from representations, determine which parts depend on earlier results, choose/describe a relationship or plan, construct a useful representation, predict/justify, and communicate a solution path.

WTC content is deliberately NOT locked to the current section or current Unit. A Unit 1 WTC may use a later-Unit Physics situation when it creates good FRQ-navigation practice. Keep the exact content I-can ID/text from the course-wide i_can_index for provenance/teacher metadata, but mark the item `coverage_role=wtc_frq_practice`; it never satisfies the current Unit's Notes/Practice/CYU/Warm-Up/Exit/Summative I-can floors. If the content has not been taught, ask students to unpack/represent/plan/predict at an accessible level rather than requiring mastery of an untaught procedure.

Rotate the FRQ moves across WTCs. Useful parts include: identify what is known/asked; annotate or interpret a diagram/graph/table; draw a force/vector/model sketch; state a relevant relationship without solving; explain which earlier part a later part depends on; make a qualitative prediction; outline an experimental/data-analysis plan; carry out a short familiar calculation; justify/interpret a result. Do not copy released/workbook questions. Use external exemplars only to learn the morphology of strong multi-part FRQs.

PHYSICS BANK FINAL HARDENING — BEFORE PM UPGRADE
---------------------------------------------------
We are deliberately stopping Framework expansion once real Bank tests are roughly 75% successful; after that, remaining math-shaped behavior belongs in Physics-specific PM upgrades. For this final Framework test, enforce these points:

• SEMANTIC CLONES: unique wording is not enough. Number swaps, object swaps, unit swaps, reordered choices, or a generic measurement wrapper do not create a new Physics question. Reuse evidence families, not prompt shells. Secure semantic-shell clones are failures.
• RELEVANT DATA ONLY: every value/detail must affect the model, inference, comparison, or calculation. Never add an unused mass/time/distance merely to manufacture variation.
• PHYSICAL PLAUSIBILITY: reject contradictory states (for example, moving at steady speed AND at rest), noun-swaps that do not fit the setup, and obvious a/an grammar residue.
• REPRESENTATION FIDELITY: if the exact I-can requires a diagram/graph/table/vector/model/ray/field/circuit, students must actually use that representation. Saying in prose what a diagram would show is not equivalent evidence.
• ANSWER-NEUTRAL DOES NOT MEAN IMAGE-FREE: build a student-safe visual; do not delete required representations to satisfy answer-neutrality.
• QUESTION DESIGN HANDOFF: every fresh Bank includes `unitN_question_design_map.json`; every canonical item carries a `question_design` object linking it to one planned slot.
• MACHINE HANDOFF: every canonical item uses the literal schema in contracts/bank_handoff_contract.json. Per-item dok, evidence_point_id, security_level, tables, figures, supporting_i_can_ids, and question_design are required; aliases do not count.
• AUDIT STATUS: every canonical item carries literal `audit_status`. Fresh builds initialize ALL items to `NEEDS AUDIT`; builder QA does not auto-promote them. Allowed later audit dispositions are KEEP, CLEAN, and REBUILD. Teacher Bank cards show destination · exact I-can ID · colored status — exact I-can statement.

STUDENT FIGURE ANSWER-NEUTRALITY — ABSOLUTE HARD RULE
-------------------------------------------------------
NEVER SHOW THE ANSWER IN THE STUDENT IMAGE.
Read `contracts/student_figure_answer_neutrality_contract.json` before authoring or reusing any student-facing visual. Inline SVG is not exempt: if an item renders inline `<svg>`, it must carry structured `figures[]` QA metadata with `render_mode=inline_svg`, a student evidence role, and answer-neutrality verification; answer-sensitive tasks also certify answer-bearing elements removed. If the student is supposed to identify, infer, rank, label, calculate, predict, critique, construct, compare, or determine a feature, the image may NOT already name, mark, highlight, draw, state, or numerically reveal that feature. Examples: do not label a force the student must identify; do not draw a direction the student must determine; do not print a value the student must calculate; do not show ranks the student must rank; do not circle the error the student must critique; do not complete a ray path/graph/circuit feature the student must construct or infer. Titles, captions, legends, and annotations count as part of the image and must also remain answer-neutral.

Hewitt captures and Framework reusable figures are NOT automatically student-safe. Inspect the actual image against the exact prompt. Crop/edit/redraw/generate an answer-neutral student version or choose a different figure when needed. Teacher keys may use separate annotated answer figures, but those must never be referenced by student items. In Banks, each figure reference must carry the required answer-neutrality verification metadata; that metadata certifies an actual visual inspection and does not replace it.
