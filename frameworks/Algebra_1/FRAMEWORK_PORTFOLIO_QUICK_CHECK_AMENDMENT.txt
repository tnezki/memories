1. CURRENT SHORT-EVIDENCE ARTIFACT
----------------------------------
The current common short-evidence artifact is QUICK CHECK.

A normal Quick Check is about four questions and common to the class.
Its architecture is slot-based rather than frozen-target-based.

  The curriculum predicts the evidence slots; the Portfolio determines the just-in-time reassessment targets; the teacher approves the actual questions.

Use curriculum/quick_check_slot_map.json as the operational slot map.
Do not revive the fixed previous-section Exit Ticket reassessment chain.

2. CURRENT PORTFOLIO STATUS
---------------------------
I Can status:
- No Evidence
- Developing
- Mastered
- Transfer

Default Mastered rule:
An I Can normally reaches Mastered when the body of evidence contains at least two independent convincing demonstrations, normally from different opportunities, with at least one reasonably current demonstration. A rich artifact may be sufficient when it contains multiple genuinely independent convincing demonstrations. Teacher professional judgment resolves mixed/unusual cases. Approved accommodations do not lower the status of valid evidence. One later weak event does not automatically erase established mastery.

Algebra Mastery Goal rule:
A Mastery Goal is demonstrated when at least 2 of its 4 I Cans are Mastered or Transfer.

Current Unit/PowerSchool scale:
- I = In Progress = 0 or 1 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated
- C = Meeting = 2 of 4
- B = Advancing = 3 of 4
- A = Mastery = 4 of 4
- A+ = Mastery plus meaningful transfer/extension evidence

A+ recognizes transfer; complete ordinary mastery is fully sufficient for A.
PowerSchool uses one living assignment per Unit; current status replaces the prior status rather than averaging attempts.
Later valid evidence may improve an earlier Unit status.

3. QUICK CHECK SLOT ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------
Use the exact current patterns in curriculum/quick_check_slot_map.json.
Summary:
- Unit 1 Section 1: two current I Cans, two questions for each.
- Unit 1 Sections 2-5: two new current + two live current-Unit reassessment slots.
- Unit 2+ Section 1: two new current + two live older-Unit reassessment slots.
- Unit 2+ Sections 2-5: two new current + one live current-Unit reassessment + one live older-Unit reassessment.

Reassessment targets are chosen from current Portfolio need at use/build time, not preassigned months ahead.

4. BANK ROLE ALIGNMENT
----------------------
The current canonical Bank is reusable question-family architecture organized:
  Mastery Goal -> exact I Can -> materially different reusable families.

The Bank supports fresh downstream authoring and practice/example access.
It does not freeze future Quick Check reassessment targets.
The Bank exposes two public views: `unitN.html` for the technical/teacher family matrix and `student.html` for student practice grouped Mastery Goal -> I Can -> Family A/B/C. Stable canonical Mastery Goal IDs, I Can IDs, and family IDs are permanent fragment anchors. Portfolio QR codes link to the exact recommended family, e.g. `student.html#U1-MG01-IC04-A`.
