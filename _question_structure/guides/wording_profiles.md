# Wording Profiles

Use after the evidence job and structure are selected.

## Standard
Givens first, question/action last.

## Inverted
Action/agent first, givens follow.

## Context-rich
Authentic situation embedded, one clear final task.

### Rules
- No fixed proportion of profiles.
- Do not force all three forms for every family.
- The wording change is secondary; the evidence variation is primary.
- In a parallel family, changing the wording profile alone does not establish semantic independence.
