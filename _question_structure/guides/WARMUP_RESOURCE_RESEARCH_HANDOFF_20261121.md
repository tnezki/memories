| Mechanism | Closest verified reference | Good brief application | Wrong application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Locate and repair a wrong step | Big Ideas A1 ch4 PDF pp.15,27,42 | Diagnose an error in the precise skill just practiced | Give same computation then append ‘explain’ |

- Provide independent verified solution, alternative valid answers, and any needed diagram.
- Test whether it can be answered without reading four paragraphs or switching to a new untaught subject.
- Check student burden: Q5/Q6 optional; deeper thinking but reasonable effort.
- Keep the bank draft until teacher accepts the family and question quality.
