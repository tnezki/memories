# Focused research: optional warm-up extensions and Smarty Pants

**November 21, 2026 — RESEARCH ONLY.** No CPM Tools, bank, lesson, CSS or published student files changed. This supplements (does not replace or add onto) the November 15 / November 21 catalog counts.

## Two different instructional jobs

| Section | Anchor | Student workload | What would make it good? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warm-up Q5 | Q3, previous completed lesson | Optional, usually 3–6 min | One new thought move: diagnose, predict, construct, compare, test a claim or transfer a representation. |
| Warm-up Q6 | Q4, previously taught Portfolio-priority skill | Optional, usually 3–6 min | Similar depth, but **requires the actual selected Q4**, so its content cannot be fixed by the current lesson number alone. |
| Smarty Pants | **Today’s** lesson skill | Optional 10–18 min (can vary) | 3–4 connected parts or a coherent mini-performance task with a decision, prediction or model that gets tested. |

**Four curated extension families per lesson TOTAL**, not four Q5 plus four Q6. An individual lesson can offer two Q5-oriented and two Q6-oriented families, but the mix depends on the real taught prerequisites and Portfolio data.

## Findings

- **Best short-extension models**: Glencoe Enrichment counterexamples and change-of-rule tasks; Big Ideas error diagnosis; Exeter inverse modeling and evidence limits; EMPower table-to-rule comparison; GSP representation changes; the constrained counting/pattern examples in the visual PODs. Use the **student action**, not the publisher wording.
- **Best Smarty Pants models**: IMP geometric rearrangement, Exeter multi-step spatial/modeling investigations, 101 Mathematical Projects data/constraint decisions, Vernier’s compare-measured-vs-model cycles, GSP predict-manipulate-conjecture tasks and coherent design comparisons.
- **Do not equate Smarty Pants with** the actual scanned book *Math for Smarty Pants*. Its playful tricks and puzzles may inspire curiosity, but after reviewing selected pages they rarely form the sustained, current-lesson, application-oriented work intended for our new section.
- **Mathercise E** has useful Reason/Solve/Sketch/Review prompt types, but its layout and independent-question mix must **not** override the approved three-page warm-up / Notability design.
- A long lab should never be copied into Q5/Q6. Instead, select one mathematical move from it, such as identify the first inconsistent data point; use the complete investigative cycle for Smarty Pants, Launch, or a lab.

## Short extension design kernels (research candidates; NOT approved families)

### ES-EXT-001: Locate the wrong partial product

- **Taught skill:** algebraic multiplication
- **Student action:** cell-level diagnosis. Given one completed row of a generic rectangle with one incorrect cell, locate and repair it without redoing the entire computation.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exploring Algebra with Geometer Sketchpad](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZtXSF-PBgdTDN2ow64cKNGFl8pzFhgz/view), PDF p. 28; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-002: Reverse engineer a missing side

- **Taught skill:** rectangle model, binomial factoring
- **Student action:** inverse construction. Provide several filled rectangle cells and one omitted side heading; recover a possible factor and check it against every cell.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exploring Algebra with Geometer Sketchpad](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZtXSF-PBgdTDN2ow64cKNGFl8pzFhgz/view), PDF p. 28; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-003: Compare without fully expanding

- **Taught skill:** multiplication of expressions
- **Student action:** structural comparison. Keep one factor fixed and change the other by one unit; predict the product change from the strip model and verify.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Carnegie Algebra 2 chapter 3 skills](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAS1oybVcyam8xQjQ/view), PDF p. 2; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-004: Show two methods agree

- **Taught skill:** expansion / representation
- **Student action:** method translation. Compare partial-products rectangle to regrouped expression; locate corresponding terms, not two full calculations.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exploring Algebra with Geometer Sketchpad](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZtXSF-PBgdTDN2ow64cKNGFl8pzFhgz/view), PDF p. 28; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-005: Find a counterexample

- **Taught skill:** properties of operations
- **Student action:** claim falsification. Judge a universal claim about a learned operation and provide a single small counterexample when false.
- **Best for:** CC3/Algebra 1, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Glencoe Algebra 1 Enrichment](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cU_TPieDT0CSLLDh48PQuEk7_JUq6gLg/view), PDF p. 7; text_sampled_in_prior_pass.

### ES-EXT-006: Choose sufficient information

- **Taught skill:** linear functions
- **Student action:** information sufficiency. From a tiny set of graphs or coordinates, identify which input determines a rule and what remains unknown.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Mathematics 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAeEs5OWp4blVGYUU/view), PDF p. 58; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-007: Interpret units, not just slope

- **Taught skill:** rates and linear equations
- **Student action:** interpretation check. Evaluate two plausible interpretations of one slope and use units/context to accept or reject them.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/Physics, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Mathematics 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAeEs5OWp4blVGYUU/view), PDF p. 40; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-008: Classify a changed rule

- **Taught skill:** patterns to tables
- **Student action:** representation comparison. One table uses constant first differences; a second does not. Decide what changed and choose a matching rule type already taught.
- **Best for:** CC3/Algebra 1, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [EMPower Seeking Patterns and Building Rules](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u4euX15PNw2a14XGpupNsuS904-VgWxG/view), PDF p. 18; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-009: Work backward from a result

- **Taught skill:** equations and function rules
- **Student action:** inverse / nonuniqueness. Supply an output or target graph feature; construct two valid inputs/models when the conditions allow.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/Precalculus, about 3-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Mathematics 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAeEs5OWp4blVGYUU/view), PDF p. 58; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-010: Check a model against evidence

- **Taught skill:** linear model / sequences
- **Student action:** model checking. Use three measured or tabular points to decide whether a proposed linear rule can fit them all; identify first mismatch.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/Physics, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Math Labs from Algebra (with career questions)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19j4LG4z61bWw8nWPMS-9RoGkYLJusAdk/view), PDF p. 8; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-011: Identify an unjustified inference

- **Taught skill:** data & graphs
- **Student action:** evidence boundary. Given a short plot or sample, distinguish an observation from an extrapolation not supported by the data.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/CC3, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Mathematics 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAeEs5OWp4blVGYUU/view), PDF p. 26; prior_page_verified.

### ES-EXT-012: Constrain an operator choice

- **Taught skill:** order of operations
- **Student action:** constraint satisfaction. Use a small fixed number set with a target value and a limited set of operations; justify one feasible construction.
- **Best for:** CC3/Algebra 1, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Visual Thinking Puzzles](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dmqgHZ_nMNecmxz62nhuuqrfAwSqWWmK/view), PDF p. 23; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-013: Count systematically

- **Taught skill:** geometric figures
- **Student action:** systematic enumeration. Instead of guessing a diagram count, organize single, combined and overlapping cases with an explicit no-duplicate criterion.
- **Best for:** CC3/Geometry, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Visual Thinking Puzzles](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dmqgHZ_nMNecmxz62nhuuqrfAwSqWWmK/view), PDF p. 23; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-014: Find the invariant

- **Taught skill:** area/perimeter
- **Student action:** invariant vs variant. Compare two differently shaped arrangements built from the same number of unit squares; decide which measure is fixed.
- **Best for:** CC3/Geometry, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [IMP More Problems of the Week](https://drive.google.com/file/d/115xibLTWmtshRkWzqsTtE6f1VfnvVi_6/view), PDF p. 35; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-015: Minimal counterexample to a pattern

- **Taught skill:** number properties
- **Student action:** generalization limits. Test a small set of cases and decide whether a proposed formula holds for all, using a reasoned limiting case.
- **Best for:** CC3/Algebra 1, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Problem of the Week](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Pv1M7SIgly7J-qQ0tdJ0vo1ZT7O96NWI/view), PDF p. 63; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-016: Change an assumption

- **Taught skill:** motion graphs / functions
- **Student action:** parameter sensitivity. Keep an original model but change direction, interval or start position; predict which graph feature changes.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/Physics, about 4-6 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Vernier Real World Math: Walk This Way](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JZCTreYmwP_8oa6Uzqr0i4j8acwVjVN-/view), original Word lab; legacy_word_text_reinspected.

### ES-EXT-017: Reconstruct the graph meaning

- **Taught skill:** motion graph
- **Student action:** cross-representation transfer. Use a short motion story and two sketches; choose the matching graph and name the feature proving it.
- **Best for:** Algebra 1/Physics, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Mathercise E](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1mm5OaAu3aL3VyPtIK7iOk6cK6feCDXg9/view), PDF p. 16; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-EXT-018: Estimate with a reasonableness bound

- **Taught skill:** modeling with quantities
- **Student action:** bound / estimate. Before precise calculation, select a plausible range and reject an answer that violates the meaning of the quantities.
- **Best for:** CC3/Algebra 1, about 3-5 minutes.
- **Evidence:** [Big Ideas Algebra 1 chapter 4](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAYmNTQ1FObXdhdWs/view), PDF p. 15; prior_page_verified.

## Smarty Pants task archetypes (today’s skill, not the Q3/Q4 spirals)

### ES-SP-001: Prototype, test, redesign

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** trinomial-by-binomial multiplication.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Build one 2x3 area model; add a one-unit strip; predict the changed product, then verify with a new expansion and a single numeric trial.
- **Coherent path:** 3-4 connected steps; same physical model; one coherent check. Recommended 10-15 min; Algebra 1 lesson 3.2.3.
- **Evidence:** [Exploring Algebra with Geometer Sketchpad](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZtXSF-PBgdTDN2ow64cKNGFl8pzFhgz/view), PDF p. 28; prior_page_verified.

### ES-SP-002: Same pieces, different borders

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** area and perimeter.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Arrange an equal set of unit squares into two outlines, compare boundary lengths and justify which arrangement uses less fencing.
- **Coherent path:** diagram -> count -> argue; no optimality claim until all candidates considered. Recommended 10-15 min; CC3/Geometry.
- **Evidence:** [IMP More Problems of the Week](https://drive.google.com/file/d/115xibLTWmtshRkWzqsTtE6f1VfnvVi_6/view), PDF p. 35; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-003: Design within a boundary

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** geometric measurement.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Propose two valid room or display layouts meeting a size rule; defend a choice using area and perimeter calculations.
- **Coherent path:** measure -> model -> evaluate tradeoff -> redesign. Recommended 12-18 min; Geometry/Algebra 1.
- **Evidence:** [101 Mathematical Projects](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xe2fwle4-Tn-PC1Ejh3xSLC0GCweif4j/view), PDF p. 22; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-004: Build and break a conjecture

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** algebraic properties.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Investigate a mathematical claim in several cases, give its most general valid statement within the taught domain, and present a counterexample to the overstatement.
- **Coherent path:** test -> revise -> justify; do not require future proof techniques. Recommended 10-14 min; CC3/Algebra 1.
- **Evidence:** [Glencoe Algebra 1 Enrichment](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cU_TPieDT0CSLLDh48PQuEk7_JUq6gLg/view), PDF p. 7; prior_page_verified.

### ES-SP-005: Identify and compare growth rules

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** function patterns.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Collect or use a short table from one process, compare a proposed linear rule with an alternative, and make a prediction with stated assumptions.
- **Coherent path:** table -> graph/rule -> evaluate -> predict. Recommended 10-15 min; Algebra 1/Precalculus.
- **Evidence:** [EMPower Seeking Patterns and Building Rules](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u4euX15PNw2a14XGpupNsuS904-VgWxG/view), PDF p. 18; prior_page_verified.

### ES-SP-006: Predict, measure, defend

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** geometry transformations.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Make a prediction about a moving construction, test it with a dynamic or static sequence, then classify what stays invariant.
- **Coherent path:** model -> test -> revise; use local interactive if available. Recommended 10-18 min; Geometry/Precalculus.
- **Evidence:** [Exploring Algebra with Geometer Sketchpad](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bZtXSF-PBgdTDN2ow64cKNGFl8pzFhgz/view), PDF p. 115; prior_page_verified.

### ES-SP-007: Three ways to locate a meeting

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** linear systems.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Use two movement datasets, predict meeting time, compare graphical intersection with algebraic solution, and identify why empirical data may differ.
- **Coherent path:** measured vs graphical vs algebraic checks; optional pre-collected data. Recommended 12-20 min; Algebra 1/Physics.
- **Evidence:** [Vernier Real World Math: Meet You at the Intersection](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qvVbQW2nYHRLOgShgI8sKaT6tA5XP1Bq/view), original Word lab; legacy_word_text_reinspected.

### ES-SP-008: Audit a changing-rate assumption

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** exponential functions.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Collect or receive rebound heights, test whether consecutive ratios are approximately constant, and compare model prediction to a later value.
- **Coherent path:** data -> ratio -> function -> model check; only after exponentials. Recommended 12-18 min; Algebra 1/Algebra 2/Physics.
- **Evidence:** [Vernier Real World Math: Bounce Back](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SA3BjHZs3Ebt59khDseGbG2JvduqdgoB/view), original Word lab; legacy_word_text_reinspected.

### ES-SP-009: Rank designs using a cost model

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** algebraic functions / economics.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Estimate the cost of two realistic school or home options using measured usage or rates; perform a sensitivity check before recommending one.
- **Coherent path:** assumptions -> computation -> decision -> limitations. Recommended 12-18 min; Algebra 1/Precalculus.
- **Evidence:** [101 Mathematical Projects](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xe2fwle4-Tn-PC1Ejh3xSLC0GCweif4j/view), PDF p. 67; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-010: Find a route under constraints

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** enumeration and representation.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Model a small journey network and compare shortest or fewest-step routes subject to one explicit restriction.
- **Coherent path:** draw network -> enumerate -> compare -> justify completeness. Recommended 10-16 min; CC3/Geometry/CS.
- **Evidence:** [101 Mathematical Projects](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xe2fwle4-Tn-PC1Ejh3xSLC0GCweif4j/view), PDF p. 46; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-011: What the measurement hides

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** physical data interpretation.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Use repeated observations to compare a prediction with actual readings; propose which source of variability most affects the conclusion.
- **Coherent path:** predicted / measured / discrepancy / next measurement. Recommended 12-18 min; Algebra 1/Physics.
- **Evidence:** [Math Labs from Algebra (with career questions)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/19j4LG4z61bWw8nWPMS-9RoGkYLJusAdk/view), PDF p. 8; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-012: Change the rule mid-investigation

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** function representations.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Analyze one real-world process before and after a condition changes, make a two-part model and test what a single uniform rule gets wrong.
- **Coherent path:** different representation not just harder arithmetic. Recommended 12-18 min; Algebra 1/Precalculus.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Mathematics 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHAeEs5OWp4blVGYUU/view), PDF p. 26; prior_page_verified.

### ES-SP-013: Evaluate a claim from mixed evidence

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** data literacy.
- **Original adaptation seed:** A team makes a numerical claim from an incomplete chart; determine what follows, what does not and what further measurement would settle it.
- **Coherent path:** interpretation -> alternative explanation -> evidence request. Recommended 10-15 min; Algebra 1/Statistics.
- **Evidence:** [101 Mathematical Projects](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1xe2fwle4-Tn-PC1Ejh3xSLC0GCweif4j/view), PDF p. 35; page_reinspected_visually.

### ES-SP-014: Use a fair rule to allocate resources

- **Current-lesson mathematics:** ratios / discrete reasoning.
- **Original adaptation seed:** Develop and compare two allocation rules for a small school resource problem, then decide what each rule treats as fair.
- **Coherent path:** ratios -> allocation -> compare -> criteria; no electoral context needed. Recommended 12-18 min; CC3/Algebra 1.
- **Evidence:** [Exeter Discrete Mathematics](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B6fznOHK0RHASUV2WGpIS0d5dUk/view), PDF p. 1; local_pdf_text_reinspected.

## One mathematically checked example (NOT a bank insertion)

For the provisional Algebra 1 Lesson 3.2.3 current-lesson task, design a mosaic whose side lengths are `(x² + 2x + 3)` and `(x + 4)` unit tiles. The school club adds one extra row of tiles without changing the first side. Students (a) construct a 2-by-3 generic rectangle, (b) predict the extra tile count without re-expanding everything, (c) verify symbolically, and (d) check a concrete case.

- Original area: `x³ + 6x² + 11x + 12`
- Expanded area: `x³ + 7x² + 13x + 15`
- Added row: `x² + 2x + 3` tiles
- At `x = 2`: `66 -> 77`, an increase of `11` tiles.

All these identities and the numeric check were **independently verified with symbolic algebra**. This sample shows coherent follow-through and meaning of an added row. It is **not** the full approved current-lesson task; check the exact I-can / student context before use.

**Separate illustrative Q5-family idea:** a flawed `(x + 3)(x + 2) = x² + 6x + 6` rectangle calculation needs one `3x + 2x = 5x` correction. A *different* family would ask how the product changes when one side grows by 1 (`+ x + 3`). Both verified; neither is an approved bank addition. **Q6 must wait for the real Portfolio Q4**, not be guessed from Lesson 3.2.3.

## Exclusions and quality gates

1. No new families declared; cross-reference the existing 390-family canonical catalog before proposing one.
2. Never promote by changing numbers or appending `Explain`. A new action must require different evidence.
3. No untaught techniques, gratuitous difficulty, long preambles, contrived contexts or recycled source figures.
4. For Q5/Q6, verify actual Q3/Q4 parent I-can, draw route and a complete solution; preserve Monday/continuation/sub sequencing.
5. For Smarty Pants, require a meaningful 3–4-step dependency: students use their earlier result to make a decision or check a model; it cannot be four independent worksheet problems.
6. All original source documents remain in Drive. Do not redistribute publisher prompts, illustrations, solutions, or teacher-only files through a public bank.
