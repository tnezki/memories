# Physics Examples

These are original demonstrations of the structure rules, not source questions.

## Same relationship, three wording profiles
Relationship: power for lifting at constant speed.

**Standard:** A crane lifts a 600 kg load upward at a constant speed of 0.50 m/s. Determine the power required.

**Inverted:** Determine the power required by a crane that lifts a 600 kg load at a constant upward speed of 0.50 m/s.

**Context-rich:** A contractor building a bridge needs a crane that can lift a 600 kg load at a constant upward speed of 0.50 m/s. Determine the required power.

## Dependent constant-rate transfer
A cyclist rides 8.0 km in 24 min at a steady pace. At the same pace, how long will a 13 km ride take?

The student must infer the rate from the first trip and use it for the second. Do not split into artificial `(a)` and `(b)` unless both intermediate and final results are separately being assessed.

## Reverse-direction family
Instead of always `distance + time -> rate`, legitimate variants may use:
- rate + time -> distance, then transfer the same rate to a new time
- distance + rate -> time, then transfer the same rate to a new distance
- two trips -> decide whether the rate is consistent, then make a prediction

## Representation-first variation
A velocity-time graph can ask students to:
- determine displacement from area
- identify intervals of positive/negative acceleration
- sketch the corresponding acceleration graph
- compare two motions using the same graph
These belong to related but not identical evidence families.
