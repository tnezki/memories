# All Classes Resources - Quick Capability Map (2026-11-21)

**Purpose:** rapid reference for designing varied warm-ups, deeper Q5/Q6 extensions, investigations, assessments and math curriculum across courses. This is a *source map*, not a question bank or endorsement of every item.

**Source boundary:** `~Curriculum / All Clasess Resources / Resources` in the connected Google Drive mirror. The directory name is spelled `All Clasess Resources` in Drive. The two extra files immediately under that parent are listed at the end.

**Inspection key:** `T` = representative text sampled in this pass; `P` = representative PDF pages visually sampled in the prior POD pass; `F` = verified file and folder inventory, but individual pages not evaluated; `M` = metadata only. A potential use is a *candidate use*, not confirmation that a specific task exists inside.

**Priority:** A = explore early for genuinely different tasks; B = useful supplement; C = lower priority or legacy. This ranks *research value for our next work*, not the quality of the material.

## Big-picture choices

- **Not all variation is worthwhile.** Kuta is good for skill variation, while Exeter, PODs and labs are better starting points for new *thinking* structures.
- **Warm-ups:** use Kuta for reliable fluency patterns, POD/Mathercise for short nonroutine prompts, and textbook workbooks for additional representations. Keep Q3/Q4 tied to taught I-cans, and make Q5/Q6 genuinely extend them.
- **Investigations and labs:** use the Algebra/Geometry math labs, Vernier real-world math, motion/rocket/car projects, and consumer-finance spreadsheets as structure references; adapt them to current equipment and lesson goals.
- **Across courses:** combine middle-grade Mathscape/EMPower prerequisites with Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Precalculus and Physics resources instead of treating each as a sealed silo.
- **Question generation:** keep the canonical Question Structures catalog and current CPM learning-goal map as authorities. These sources suggest structures; do not copy published questions/figures or import legacy databases directly.

## What this pass establishes - and does not

- **73 concise capability records** across 9 groups. The main Resources directory has eight top-level collections; dozens of subfolders were inventoried, and representative actual PDFs were checked where text was available.
- **Verified text samples:** Exeter Mathematics 1/2, Kuta Polynomial Operations, and the saved Unit Circle Lessons. **Prior POD pass:** representative pages of the scanned puzzle/problem books and Mathercise collections. All other entries are clearly identified from the actual names and folder contents, not falsely reported as page-by-page reads.
- **Legacy cautions:** old `.bnk` databases, executables, image scans, archived `.doc`/`.ppt` files, and chapter ZIPs may need later format conversion. The words "teacher" and "test bank" may indicate material that is not suitable for direct student distribution.
- **Not a full extraction:** chapter pages, every question, lab instructions, licensing rights, and scanned-file content have *not* been completely indexed.

## Research sequence that would pay off next

1. Sample **Exeter Mathematics 1-3** and **IMP/POD** for short nonroutine structures that directly extend taught skills; do not merely add "explain" to routine problems.
2. Sample **Algebra/Geometry Math Labs**, **Vernier Real World Math** and the **car, bounceball, rocket, finance** files for repeatable lab templates and inquiry launches.
3. Use **Texas Student Workbook enrichment/word-problem pages** and **Big Ideas/Carnegie** for alternate representations and legitimate assessment evidence.
4. Review **middle-school EMPower/Mathscape/AGS** for prerequisite repair and accessible scaffolding, not as a way to automatically lower expectations.
5. Convert only the best distinct patterns into the established canonical Question Structures workflow, aligned to the current course and learning goal; teacher review remains required.

No original textbooks, copyrighted problem statements, student records, or lesson/bank edits are included in this transfer.
