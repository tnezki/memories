# Algebra 1 warm-up source-bank completion — November 21, 2026

## Source-lesson architecture (LOCKED for this bank)

A lesson **contributes** two Q3/Q4 source spiral families after that lesson is taught. Each source family has four independent optional extension approaches and eight prepared numerical/structural versions. A future lesson selects a Q3 question from its most recently completed lesson, then Q5 from that Q3's four options. Its Q4 comes from an eligible earlier taught skill (balanced-random default for prebuilding); Q6 follows that chosen Q4 source family. The teacher can later request a Portfolio-priority Q4/Q6 replacement as one matched pair. The bank does NOT pick the source for each future lesson; that remains assembler work.

**Q5/Q6 are student-optional, and each may use ONE of its four linked options.** Smarty Pants remains a distinct section for today's learning, not this prior-skill bank.

## Review protocol

1. Open **Algebra 1 Warm-up Families → Q5/Q6 Extensions**.
2. Choose the lesson that **taught** the source skill. For lesson 3.2.3's future warm-up, inspect source lesson 3.2.3; for 3.2.3's Q3, inspect 3.2.2.
3. Inspect both source spiral families, their four extension choices each, and cycle matched numbered versions.
4. Mark **Approve** or **Needs Work** (with a specific note); export reviews. Existing Q1/Q2 and Q3/Q4 review flags and approved assessment banks are not modified.
5. Do not treat current teacher-review drafts as automatically approved for publishing.
