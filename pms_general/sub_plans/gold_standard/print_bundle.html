STEM Current Challenge

Water Rocket Challenge

The challenge: Design, build, test, and improve a water rocket that stays in the air as long as possible.

Students are working on their current challenge and know what to do. They should continue building/design work and keep their Engineering Design Notebook current. Testing should happen only when students can safely follow the established class routine.

Core requirements

  • Compatible with the class launcher.
  • At least two stabilizing fins and an aerodynamic nose cone.
  • A parachute or recovery system.
  • Multiple test launches documented with conditions, results, observations, and design changes.
  • Notebook evidence should show research, sketches, decisions, photos, data, failures, changes, and learning.
