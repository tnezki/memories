3. CONTENT-STABLE BANK FINGERPRINT — HARD HANDOFF CONTRACT
======================================================================

The 4A map and 4B build MUST identify the Bank by curriculum/content identity, not by ZIP container bytes. 4B MUST run its bundled Tools/stable_bank_fingerprint.py against the current extracted/materialized Bank root and compare the returned method/value to the map.

SUPPORTED METHODS

A. stage3e-canonical-v1
- Read current Bank STAGE_3E_HANDOFF.json.
- Validate canonical_authored_content_sha256 as 64 hexadecimal characters.
- Compute current value as:
    stage3e-canonical:<lowercase-64-hex>
- Compare exactly to the map's source_bank_fingerprint.

B. content-manifest-v1
- Extract/use the Bank contents.
- Ignore __MACOSX/, .DS_Store, AppleDouble files (._*), __pycache__/, and *.pyc.
- Normalize relative paths to POSIX forward slashes.
- SHA-256 every regular file.
- Sort by normalized relative path.
- Feed UTF-8(path) + NUL + lowercase_file_sha256 + LF for each record into SHA-256.
- Compute current value as:
    content-manifest:<lowercase-64-hex>
- Compare exactly to the map's source_bank_fingerprint.

LEGACY / INVALID
- A source_bank_fingerprint in raw-ZIP form sha256:<...> is NOT a supported 4A→4B provenance identity. Fail closed and request one rerun of 4A under the current PM.
- Do not silently waive a real content mismatch.
- Do not ask the teacher to rerun 4A merely because the Control Panel repackaged unchanged Bank files into a different ZIP container.

4. CORE PHYSICS NOTES DESIGN
======================================================================

Physics Notes should read like an illustrated conceptual-physics chapter that periodically stops for students to process and apply ideas.

The middle of the Notes is NOT a math worksheet with paragraphs inserted between questions.
It is also NOT a wall of textbook prose.

Execute the mapped story as:

  conceptual reading / phenomenon / model
  -> selected source image(s) and explanation
  -> processing anchor
       * mapped EX/YTI when that is the anchor
       * mapped Stop & Discuss otherwise
  -> next conceptual beat

Use the map's exact sequence unless the current Framework requires a fixed outer shell around it.

READING
- Write multiple short, developed paragraphs when the mapped depth is developed/substantial.
- Explain what changes, what stays the same, what causes what, which evidence matters, and how representations relate.
- Use concrete Physics language appropriate for the course.
- Avoid one-sentence stacking.
- Avoid decorative stories or generic filler.
- Source provenance guides authoring; do not write student-facing phrases such as "Hewitt says" or "the Bank says" unless attribution is instructionally necessary.
- Paraphrase protected source text. Do not reproduce long textbook passages or captions verbatim.

IMAGE-DRIVEN STORY
- Use every image selected by the approved map unless the actual source file is corrupt/unusable; fail closed if that happens.
- Images are part of the explanation, not decoration.
- Around each image, write enough prose to direct attention to the mapped what_students_should_notice without simply giving away a later question answer.
- Several images may appear in one story beat when the map intentionally sequences/composes them.
- Do not ration images to make the page shorter.

KWL-STYLE OPENING — HARD PHYSICS NOTES RULE
- Begin the section with a simple prior-knowledge/wonder activation, not an abstract prediction-of-relationship task.
- Use the student-facing labels **What I already know** and **What I wonder**.
- Preferred direction: "Skim the headings and figures. Write one thing you already know about this topic and one question you have."
- Do NOT ask students to invent an "expected relationship" before the conceptual story has developed enough context.
- If a reflection such as **What I figured out** is used, place it near the end of the section after the conceptual story/Summary work, not as a third empty opening box.
- The KWL activation is a brief orientation task. It does not replace Vocabulary Preview, WTC, mapped EX/YTI, or mapped Stop & Discuss anchors.

======================================================================
7. STOP & DISCUSS
======================================================================

Stop & Discuss exists only where 4A mapped it because the preceding reading/visual beat needs processing and no mapped EX/YTI naturally fills that role.

Write the final prompt from the map's discussion_job and expected reasoning.

GOOD S&D BEHAVIOR
- concise;
- specific to the immediately preceding reading/image/model;
- asks students to compare, predict, explain, rank, infer, identify evidence, or resolve a misconception as mapped;
- normally 1–3 focused prompts depending on the mapped job/Framework.

BAD S&D BEHAVIOR
- generic "What do you notice?" without a specified relationship/evidence target;
- repeating the same stock comprehension shell;
- inventing an extra S&D around every EX/YTI;
- turning S&D into a hidden second question bank.

When a mapped visual drives the discussion, keep the visual visible/near the prompt.

10. VOCABULARY — PREVIEW + ACTIVE SORT + DEVELOPMENT
======================================================================

Follow the mapped vocabulary sequence and current Framework. The Physics Framework's "terms only" preview means no definitions are given at the beginning; it does NOT mean the preview should be passive. Use the established three-column vocabulary sort as the active prior-knowledge task.

HARD OPENING VOCABULARY CONTRACT
- Preview the exact mapped terms near the beginning WITHOUT definitions.
- Immediately below the term list, include the established three-column vocabulary sort.
- Use this student-facing direction exactly unless the user explicitly changes it:
  "Sort the vocabulary. Write each vocabulary word in the column that best matches what you know right now. You may move a word later."
- Use these three headers exactly:
  1. Know for sure
  2. Recognize
  3. Don't know yet
- The sort is prior-knowledge activation. Students write/copy the vocabulary words into the columns; do not pre-sort the words for them.

REQUIRED MARKUP
  <section class="vocab-preview-section">
    <h2 class="h-vocab">Vocabulary Preview</h2>
    <p class="vocab-list">...</p>
    <div class="vocab-sort-task">
      <p class="vocab-sort-directions">Sort the vocabulary. Write each vocabulary word in the column that best matches what you know right now. You may move a word later.</p>
      <table class="vocab-sort-table">
        <thead><tr><th>Know for sure</th><th>Recognize</th><th>Don't know yet</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody><tr class="vocab-sort-space"><td></td><td></td><td></td></tr></tbody>
      </table>
    </div>
  </section>

CLASS PLACEMENT IS HARD
- `vocab-sort-table` belongs on the TABLE.
- `vocab-sort-space` belongs on the blank BODY ROW (`tr`).
- directions use `vocab-sort-directions` and sit inside `vocab-sort-task`.
- Do not shrink or remove the blank sort row; the current notes.css owns its writing height.
- Run bundled Tools/fix_notes_layout_contract.py before final QA to catch stale/misplaced vocabulary-sort classes.

VOCABULARY DEVELOPMENT
- At each term's first meaningful introduction in prose, bold it once.
- Develop meaning through the conceptual story/images rather than only through a glossary.
- Immediately before Summary, include the mapped concise Vocabulary with Definitions reference.
- Do not bold every recurrence.
- Do not pad the preview with familiar words merely to fill a template.
