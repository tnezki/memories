BUILD NOTES MAP — STEP 4A / BANK-V2
====================================

STATUS: CURRENT
ROLE: Build one complete Unit Notes map from the current Course Framework, finalized Bank-v2 Notes anchors, and the Framework-declared Notes instructional source/spine.

4A is MAP / CUT / ROUTE.
It does NOT author final Notes HTML.
It does NOT audit or rebuild the Bank.
It does NOT redesign the established Notes format.

For Physics, the current Framework declares the Physics textbook as the Notes instructional spine and the Physics Notes Image Toolkit as a course-owned reference-in-place resource.

======================================================================
6. SOURCE CHUNK CONTRACT
======================================================================

For every relevant source block assign one:

KEEP
- directly useful for the exact LT/I-cans;
- preserve source idea/order and recognizable explanatory language;
- 4B trims mainly by omission/light editing.

CONDENSE
- useful but too long/repetitive for the lesson;
- preserve conceptual sequence and useful source language;
- remove repeated examples/details/tangents first.

DROP
- outside current target;
- redundant for the lesson;
- media/navigation/answer clutter;
- unnecessary tangent;
- material whose inclusion would damage the intended classroom flow.

For every retained reading chunk record:
- flow_id;
- source_file;
- source heading/span description;
- action = KEEP|CONDENSE;
- text_fidelity = CLOSE|LIGHT_CONDENSE|STRONG_CONDENSE;
- transition_heading_target;
- purpose;
- i_can_ids;
- retained_images;
- estimated_minutes or concise pacing intent when useful.

Default toward CLOSE or LIGHT_CONDENSE.
STRONG_CONDENSE should be uncommon and purposeful.

Do not write final reading prose into the map.

8. WTC + EXAMPLE/YTI — EXACT BANK ROUTING
======================================================================

Example/YTI:

Do not:
- invent a replacement pair;
- rewrite/split a canonical pair;
- pull a pair from Practice or another destination;
- use old Bank-stage outputs.

9. STOP & DISCUSS / REFERENCE-NOTE MAPPING
======================================================================

Follow the current Course Framework/profile.

For current Physics, substantial instructional reading must receive content-specific sense-making consistent with the Physics Notes profile.

A mapped Stop & Discuss should identify:
- after_flow_id;
- i_can_ids;
- discussion_job;
- concise prompt skeletons, not polished final prose;
- source image/representation IDs when relevant;
- expected reasoning/evidence to listen for.

Prompts must be specific to the immediately preceding reading/representation.
Avoid stock comprehension questions.

Map a Physics Note only when it serves a real instructional job such as:
- making a relationship explicit;
- interpreting an equation/model;
- clarifying a representation convention;
- connecting a figure to physical meaning;
- distinguishing key vocabulary.

Record the job/source basis; 4B writes the final wording.

Do not map reference blocks merely to fill space.
