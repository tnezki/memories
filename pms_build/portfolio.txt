NORMAL TEACHER WORKFLOW
-----------------------

======================================================================
8. ITEM-LEVEL EVIDENCE STRENGTH — INTERNAL
======================================================================

Use exactly:
- `UNUSABLE`
- `LIMITED`
- `PARTIAL`
- `CONVINCING`
- `STRONG_TRANSFER`

Interpretation:
UNUSABLE — no defensible target judgment from this response.
LIMITED — major error/misconception; little valid evidence of intended target.
PARTIAL — meaningful correct thinking but incomplete/inconsistent/undermined by an important gap.
CONVINCING — substantially demonstrates the intended target; minor slips may remain if understanding is still clear.
STRONG_TRANSFER — convincing plus meaningful transfer, connection, critique, modeling, strategic representation use, or successful less-routine application.

Do not convert these labels to points.
Do not penalize a response for reasoning/representation that the task did not ask for.

Useful error classes when supported:
  execution_slip
  procedural_gap
  conceptual_misconception
  representation_error
  reasoning_gap
  interpretation_context_error
  communication_notation_issue
  no_usable_evidence

======================================================================
10. CURRENT I CAN STATUS — HARD CURRENT MODEL
======================================================================

Student-facing/status symbols:
  ○ No Evidence
  ◐ Developing
  ● Mastered
  ★ Transfer

Use CSS-drawn same-size status markers in PDF templates for ○/◐/● so glyph/font metrics do not create inconsistent circle sizes. The star may remain a symbol.

NO EVIDENCE
No usable demonstrated evidence is currently available for that I Can.

DEVELOPING
Some usable evidence exists, but mastery is not yet established.
A single routine `CONVINCING` response normally remains Developing.

MASTERED — DEFAULT RULE
An I Can reaches `● Mastered` when the body of evidence contains at least TWO independent convincing demonstrations, normally from different opportunities, with at least one reasonably current demonstration.

A rich artifact may be sufficient when it contains multiple genuinely independent convincing demonstrations within the artifact.
Approved accommodations do not lower the status of valid evidence.
Teacher judgment resolves mixed/unusual cases.

Do NOT automatically lower an established Mastered status because of one later weak response. Record the contradiction and recommend another look when warranted.

TRANSFER
`★ Transfer` means the I Can is already securely mastered AND the body of evidence includes meaningful `STRONG_TRANSFER` evidence. Transfer is not awarded merely for extra work, more words, or a harder-looking page.

LOW-CONFIDENCE/AMBIGUOUS evidence cannot automatically produce a consequential status change.

11. ALGEBRA 1 MASTERY GOAL + UNIT STATUS
======================================================================

Current Algebra architecture is 4 Mastery Goals per unit, 4 I Cans per Mastery Goal.

MASTERY GOAL RULE — LOCKED FOR CURRENT ALGEBRA 1:
A Mastery Goal is DEMONSTRATED when at least 2 of its 4 I Cans are `● Mastered` or `★ Transfer`.

UNIT / POWERSCHOOL SCALE — LOCKED FOR CURRENT ALGEBRA 1:
- `I` = In Progress: 0 or 1 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated
- `C` = Meeting: 2 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated
- `B` = Advancing: 3 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated
- `A` = Mastery: 4 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated
- `A+` = Mastery ★: 4 of 4 Mastery Goals demonstrated PLUS meaningful transfer/extension evidence

A+ is recognition of meaningful transfer; ordinary complete mastery is fully sufficient for A.
Do not create an early F from missing/early evidence.

For courses other than Algebra 1, DO NOT assume this 2-of-4 / I-C-B-A-A+ rule unless the current course Framework/Portfolio policy explicitly adopts it. Resolve the current course-specific rollup or keep the report at I Can/Mastery Goal evidence level and flag missing rollup policy.

14. CLASS & INTERVENTION SUMMARY PDF
======================================================================

Produce ONE current teacher-facing PDF in:
  04 Class & Intervention Summaries/

The purpose is rapid analysis and intervention planning, not a decorative report.

For a typical 4-question Quick Check affecting four I Cans, use this organization:

PAGE 1 — COMBINED ALGEBRA 1 / COURSE SUMMARY
- all affected I Cans on ONE page when reasonably possible;
- each I Can card shows counts/percentage by internal evidence strength plus no submission;
- list the students who still need stronger/additional evidence;
- group student names under period headers (e.g. `3rd Hour` first, then `6th Hour`);
- use bulleted name lists in 2 or 3 columns depending on fit;
- first name + last initial is acceptable to conserve space;
- do not repeat `(3rd)` / `(6th)` after every student name.

PAGE 2+ — INDIVIDUAL CLASS BREAKDOWNS
- one page per class when the affected I Can count is about four;
- keep ALL four I Can cards on the same page when they fit;
- include actual student names, not just counts;
- favor compact readable columns over splitting two I Cans per page.

FINAL PAGE — COMBINED INTERVENTION GROUPS
Do NOT make separate intervention pages by class.
Combine available students across periods, but show period headers so the teacher can locate them.
Include a clear key:

`Reteach / targeted practice`
  Evidence shows a substantial gap, misconception, or unusable response; instruction/practice is likely needed before another attempt.

`Brief guided practice`
  Student showed part of the idea but not enough yet; likely needs a short example, prompt, or check-in rather than a full reteach.

`Ready for independent reassessment`
  Student already has a convincing demonstration but has not yet satisfied the independent body-of-evidence rule; no reteach is implied — the student needs another independent opportunity.

`No evidence / absent`
  No usable evidence from the relevant opportunity; the student needs a first/current evidence opportunity.

When useful, also identify students already Mastered/Transfer who should not be pulled for routine reteaching.

ALGEBRA INTERVENTION CONTEXT:
The teacher has a 29-minute intervention period Tuesday-Friday. Thursday is guaranteed math; other days may also be available. Use this context to make the groupings practical, but do NOT create a day-by-day schedule unless explicitly requested.

======================================================================
15. NEXT-TARGET / INTERVENTION LOGIC
======================================================================

Choose student `next best target` using the CURRENT body of evidence, not simply the last wrong answer.
Prefer targets that are:
- aligned to the current/nearby unit;
- blocking a Mastery Goal from becoming demonstrated;
- already close to Mastered and needing another independent opportunity; OR
- showing a clear misconception that merits targeted support.

Do not send a secure/mastered student back to pointless routine repetition.
When all nearby routine targets are secure, consider a legitimate Transfer/extension opportunity.

Class intervention categories are instructional recommendations, not proficiency labels.

Safe subset processing is allowed: process defensible records and isolate ambiguous cases in `review_queue.csv` rather than guessing.

A review case must not silently block unrelated students from receiving updated reports.
