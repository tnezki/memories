===========================================================
5. INTERNAL EVIDENCE STRENGTH
===========================================================
Use exactly UNUSABLE / LIMITED / PARTIAL / CONVINCING / STRONG_TRANSFER.

CONVINCING: substantially demonstrates target; minor slip may remain if understanding is still clear.
PARTIAL: meaningful correct target thinking is visible, but incomplete/inconsistent or one significant error prevents a complete demonstration.
LIMITED: some target-relevant work exists, but major error/misconception means little valid demonstration.
UNUSABLE: blank/irrelevant/unreadable/no defensible target judgment.
STRONG_TRANSFER: convincing plus meaningful transfer/connection/critique/modeling/less-routine application.

Judge only what the task asks. Do not convert to points.
