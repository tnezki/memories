GLOBAL WRITING RULES
--------------------
SHORT BY DEFAULT.
Extra context is allowed only when it improves the Physics or carries evidence. Do not manufacture a new lead-in sentence merely to make a parallel look different.

PARALLEL IS INTENTIONAL.
A declared parallel should preserve the mapped skill, evidence job, approximate difficulty, and structural family while changing only legitimate variation axes. Similarity is expected. A noun swap alone is not enough when the map calls for a deeper variation, but novelty is not the goal.

VISUALS ARE CHEAP, NOT A QUOTA.
When a graph, FBD, vector diagram, motion diagram, table, or model materially improves the question, make a purpose-built visual/data instance. Do not ration visuals merely to save work. Exact visual uniqueness is NOT a pass/fail metric: the same correct visual may legitimately serve more than one question. Judge correctness and usefulness, not a uniqueness count.

PHYSICS BEFORE ALGEBRA SHELLS.
Quantitative prompts use concise physical situations. A formula may be provided when appropriate. Do not write naked variable-assignment prompts.

NO UPSTREAM REPAIRS IN THIS STAGE.
Stage 3B preserves Stage 3A canonical item objects and figures. If a Stage 3A seed appears to have an answer-key or wording defect but its prompt/map relationship is still usable, record it in STAGE_3B_REPORT.txt under UPSTREAM PATCH NOTES and continue. Do not copy a suspected incorrect answer into a new variant: independently solve every new Stage 3B item. Fail closed only if an upstream seed is too ambiguous or broken to parallel safely.

PRACTICE SET 2 / 3 / EXTRA CONTRACT
-----------------------------------
The audited map owns each slot. Use Practice Set 1 as the canonical seed family.

Vocabulary families:
- Practice Set 1 may intentionally contain a MIX of approved vocabulary structures.
- For each vocabulary seed, later Practice forms should intentionally rotate into other approved vocabulary directions when available, such as term->definition, definition->term, concept/example->term, and term->concept/example.
- Do not require one single vocabulary direction for all questions in a set.
- Avoid repeating the PS1 direction for a family when another mapped/approved direction is available, unless the audited map explicitly calls for the repetition.
- Matching may be used only where the map/Framework allows it as a presentation mode.

Conceptual / representation families:
- Preserve the seed evidence job and approximate difficulty.
- Legitimate variation may change situation, givens, evidence set, misconception, prediction target, representation data, or requested inference.
- Keep prompts compact. Do not force explanation/justification wrappers onto every question.

Physics-math families:
- Preserve the mapped direct / inverse / representation-data / dependent-multistep family job.
- Vary values, givens, unknown, situation, data, representation, or dependency appropriately.
- Do NOT force wording profiles by Practice-set number. In particular, Extra Practice is not automatically context-rich.
- Context-rich is a tool only when it improves the Physics.

PRACTICE COMPOSITION
--------------------
The familiar 10 vocabulary + 10 conceptual + 4 Physics-math pattern is a strong DEFAULT, not an inflexible quota. The audited map owns the actual section composition. Do not fail or rewrite a valid mapped section merely because it resolves to a small purposeful deviation such as 11/9/4.

CYU CONTRACT
------------
- Author the mapped six CYU items per section when that is the current architecture.
- CYU is a short check of what students should now be able to do.
- Adapt/select strong mapped Practice families rather than inventing six unrelated structures.
- Use concise calculate, identify, interpret, compare, graph/diagram, or brief-explanation jobs as mapped.
- CYU may intentionally resemble Practice because its purpose is to check recently learned work.

WARM-UP CONTRACT
----------------
Use the established four-slot Physics Warm-Up pattern for each mapped Warm-Up:
- Q1: current/recent Physics retrieval;
- Q2: older/spiraled Physics retrieval;
- Q3: concise conceptual, vocabulary, or application retrieval;
- Q4: recurring Physics math/formula work in a short physical situation.

Warm-Up repetition is legitimate. Do not force artificial novelty or representation quotas. The Q4 math slot follows the Physics-before-algebra-shell rule: the physical situation supplies the measurements and the student uses/rearranges/interprets the relationship as needed.
