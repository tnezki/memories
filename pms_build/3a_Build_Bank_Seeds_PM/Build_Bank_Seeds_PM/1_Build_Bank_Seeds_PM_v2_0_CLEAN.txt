GLOBAL QUESTION-WRITING RULES
-----------------------------
SHORT BY DEFAULT.
A question gets extra context only when context improves the Physics. Context-rich is an available structure, not
the default voice. Remove sentences that do not change the physical reasoning or evidence job.

VISUALS ARE EFFECTIVELY FREE.
If a graph, FBD, vector diagram, motion diagram, table, model, or other representation materially improves the
question or is required by the audited map, CREATE A PURPOSE-BUILT ONE. Do not reuse an existing visual across
separate canonical questions merely to save work. A connected multipart WTC may intentionally share one stimulus.
A later parallel question normally receives its own corresponding fresh visual/data.

PHYSICS BEFORE ALGEBRA SHELLS.
Quantitative questions begin with a concise physical situation that naturally supplies the measurements. A formula
may be given when appropriate. Students may use, choose, rearrange, invert, interpret, or combine a relationship.
Do not write naked variable-assignment prompts such as "d=18 m, t=3 s; find v."

QUESTION STRUCTURE MUST SERVE THE MAP.
Do not add Student A/B wrappers, generic justify prompts, decorative contexts, or extra representations merely to
look rigorous or varied. Direct questions are allowed. Intentional parallelism is allowed. The audited map owns the
planned structure and evidence job.

INTERNAL LABELS NEVER REACH STUDENTS.
Do not expose terms such as seed, parallel, family, architecture, evidence job, map slot, wording profile, or audit.

DOK/BLOOM AFTER AUTHORING.
Do not generate question prose backward from a desired metadata label.

STAGE 3A DESTINATION CONTRACTS
------------------------------
A. WTC
- Author the mapped one-per-section WTC slots.
- WTC is FRQ-style decomposition/navigation practice.
- Content may come from outside the current section or current Unit when the audited map/Framework permits it.
- Use one coherent shared situation/stimulus with 3-5 genuinely connected parts.
- Parts may interpret, plan/model, calculate/analyze, justify, critique, or translate representations.
- If the WTC refers to a graph/table/diagram/model, provide the actual representation.
- WTC does not satisfy current-Unit I-can coverage floors merely by existing.

B. EXAMPLE / YOU TRY IT
- Author every mapped Example and YTI pair.
- Example is the instructional seed.
- YTI is a deliberate parallel/application of that Example, not an unrelated novelty question.
- When an I-can naturally supports quantitative Physics, strong quantitative applications take precedence over
  generic vocabulary/conceptual filler.
- Keep both concise.
- If a visual improves the evidence, generate a purpose-built visual for each canonical item rather than sharing one
  only to save production time.

C. PRACTICE SET 1
- Practice Set 1 is the seed Practice form.
- Execute the audited map's 24 planned slots per section when that is the current map architecture.
- For the current Physics architecture, the expected default composition is:
    Q1-Q10  = vocabulary families
    Q11-Q20 = conceptual / representation families
    Q21-Q24 = Physics math
- The audited map, not this reminder, owns the exact term/I-can/structure assigned to each slot.

Vocabulary seed rules:
- Use the mapped important terms.
- Normally 1-2 questions per important term until the ten vocabulary slots are filled.
- Keep wording compact.
- Stage 3B will invert/transform these families across later Practice forms using approved vocabulary directions.

Conceptual seed rules:
- Use concise predict, compare, classify, misconception, cause/effect, evidence/model, graph/diagram interpretation,
  or brief explanation structures as mapped.
- A representation is part of the question when it materially carries the evidence; do not describe an unseen graph
  or diagram in prose.
- Do not turn every conceptual question into an explanation paragraph.

Physics-math seed rules:
- Q21 normally serves a contextual direct quantitative job.
- Q22 normally serves an inverse/rearranged relationship job.
- Q23 normally serves another useful quantitative application or representation/data-to-calculation job.
- Q24 normally serves a genuinely DEPENDENT multi-step job: an earlier result is required to obtain the final result.
- When the section naturally supports quantitative Physics, use its important physical relationships.
- When it does not, preserve Physics-math practice using an accessible physical situation and an appropriate supplied
  relationship as needed. Do not substitute naked algebra drills.

PRACTICE PURPOSE
----------------
Practice is a rich instructional menu and a source pool for downstream Activities. The 24 seed questions per section
are not 24 separate assessment evidence jobs, and the eventual 96 Practice questions per section are not a 96-question
student assignment. Repetition and parallel families are legitimate when they serve learning.
