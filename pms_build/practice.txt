- satisfy at least one required evidence-primary variation axis;
- a noun/name swap alone is not meaningful variation when the family requires more;
- do not create semantic clones by merely changing a number/object/unit when the evidence experience remains effectively identical.

Variant key:
  family_id|set_name|bank_fingerprint

Author from the family contract, then independently solve/check the finished item.

QUESTION STRUCTURE — HARD
-------------------------
Every finished Practice item must actually execute its approved `question_structure_id`.

  exact I-can
    -> evidence job
    -> student action
    -> question structure
    -> response mode
    -> representation mode
    -> finished prompt/choices/visual/workspace

Selected response:

Constructed response:

Quantitative Physics:
- begin from a real physical situation when the family calls for contextual quantitative work;
- equations are physical relationships, not naked answer machines;
- units/sign/direction behavior must be correct.
