MATH SUMMATIVE SHELL-CLASS CONTRACT — HARD:
  - Question size is a student WORKSPACE decision, never a DOK/difficulty label and never a default. Do not assign `q-standard` merely because no layout decision was made.
  - `q-compact`: brief direct work / short response with little written footprint.
  - `q-standard`: moderate computation or short explanation with a few steps.
  - `q-large`: multi-step work, modeling, interpretation, critique/justification, student graphing, or meaningful representation + written reasoning.
  - `q-xlarge`: genuinely extended response/large work area when page composition supports it.
  - DETERMINISTIC FIRST PASS: short direct algebra/evaluation items remain `q-compact` even when they ask for one brief check/property/sentence of support; moderate contextual equations/multi-step computation/short analytical responses normally use `q-standard`; use `q-large` when the response footprint is clearly larger (analyze/explain a content-bearing graph/table, write an equation from a representation with evidence, create a representation plus context/explanation, evaluate a claim with evidence, or substantial contextual verification); reserve `q-xlarge` for genuinely extended work. Do not begin from `q-standard` and adjust only exceptions.
  - Reasoning verbs alone do not force `q-large`; estimate handwriting footprint. Page composition may increase space. If an obvious compact item is intentionally enlarged, add a hidden `data-workspace-override` reason so the choice is auditable rather than accidental.
