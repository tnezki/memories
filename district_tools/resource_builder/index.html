    What happens next?

    1

    Choose the resource and complete only the teacher-facing controls that matter.

    2

    Download the request ZIP and upload it to ChatGPT. The ZIP contains the full build instructions; no separate prompt is needed.

    3

    Receive one response ZIP. Unzip it and open CLICK_ME.html to reach the finished resource, teacher materials, and QA.

    Built-in safeguards

    The request carries the district response standard plus the selected resource profile. It requires accurate MathJax, real graphs/diagrams when needed, locked CSS, finished PDFs/native files, conflict handling, and visual QA.

    Files: selected files are packaged locally in your browser. This page does not upload them by itself. Follow district policy for any protected or student data you later send to an AI service.
