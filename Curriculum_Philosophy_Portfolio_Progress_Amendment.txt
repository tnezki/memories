CURRICULUM PHILOSOPHY AMENDMENT - PORTFOLIO PROGRESS / GRADING
===============================================================
STATUS: CURRENT
SCOPE: universal Portfolio evidence-growth philosophy, class-opportunity timing, family-facing progress language, and PowerSchool consequence boundary.
REVISION: 2026-09-29 - PROGRESS CONTINUUM + FALLING-BEHIND / F SEPARATION

PURPOSE
-------
This amendment extends the current Portfolio / recurring-evidence Philosophy. It supersedes older Portfolio language only where that language conflicts with the current progress model declared by:
  Tools/PORTFOLIO_PROGRESS_MODEL_CONTRACT.json

1. EVIDENCE GROWS ALONG A CONTINUUM
-----------------------------------
Portfolio should distinguish no opportunity, unsuccessful/absent evidence, meaningful partial understanding, one solid demonstration, repeated solid demonstrations, and extension. Do not collapse all non-perfect work into one weak category.

The family-facing I Can continuum is:
- Not Yet Assessed
- Started
- Developing
- Progressing
- Mastered
- Extended

The exact meanings and deterministic runtime rules are owned by the shared Portfolio Progress Model contract.

2. PARTIAL UNDERSTANDING COUNTS
-------------------------------
Evidence is interpreted according to what the student actually understands. A secondary representation/detail error does not erase defensible evidence of the core idea.

Example: if a student repeatedly identifies an x-intercept as 3 when the prompt also asks for (3, 0), that is meaningful evidence of intercept identification. The ordered-pair representation remains a gap, but the response is not treated as zero understanding. Repeated meaningful partial evidence may advance the student toward Progressing under the shared contract.

3. CLASS OPPORTUNITY AND STUDENT PERFORMANCE ARE DIFFERENT
----------------------------------------------------------
Portfolio must track whether the class has had an assessment opportunity separately from whether one individual student produced usable evidence. A student absence may leave the student's evidence weak while the class-opportunity clock still advances.

Direct teacher observations may strengthen a student's body of evidence, but they do not by themselves advance the class-opportunity clock used for absence/F timing.

4. FALLING BEHIND IS A SUPPORT SIGNAL
-------------------------------------
`Falling Behind` means the class has moved through enough opportunities that the student needs catch-up/support. It does NOT automatically mean F.

After at least two class assessment opportunities for a Mastery Goal:
- below-C evidence with meaningful growth may report Falling Behind while PowerSchool remains blank;
- F is reserved for no meaningful growth / missing or unusable evidence across that opportunity window.

Before the two-opportunity threshold, a below-C Mastery Goal does not receive an I or an F in PowerSchool.

5. NO I GRADE IN FAMILY REPORTS OR POWERSCHOOL
----------------------------------------------
The former visible/exported I / In Progress grade is retired. Internal routing logic may use private implementation codes, but the family report and PowerSchool export must never surface I as the Mastery Goal grade.

6. CURRENT GRADES REMAIN CHANGEABLE
-----------------------------------
C/B/A/A+ and F are current evidence states, not permanent labels. New evidence can change the current result. Portfolio does not average attempts into a permanent penalty.

7. REPORT COMMUNICATION
-----------------------
Preserve the approved recurring student report. Show the six progress meanings in one compact text-only Status guide; use a five-segment progress bar + short label on each I Can; show one equal-size status/grade box on each Mastery Goal card; remove the old circle/star and visible per-I-Can Next line.

The Recent evidence section shows the four most recent assessed evidence events in true chronological ledger order. Older evidence remains in private Portfolio history.

All other current Portfolio Philosophy rules remain in force.

END - PORTFOLIO PROGRESS / GRADING AMENDMENT
