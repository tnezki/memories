Major Strengths

  • Students generate multiple design ideas and identify useful research/test information.
  • Many students show a complete iterative design process.
  • Several students connect redesign choices to observed prototype behavior.

Top Actionable Patterns

  • Use evidence to compare alternatives before choosing.
  • Distinguish a brainstorm list from an evidence-based design decision.
  • Explain why a redesign should address the observed failure.
Highest-leverage class target: Use research/test evidence to compare alternatives and defend the final redesign.

Suggested Instructional Groupings

Decision-from-evidence group

Shaggy Rogers, Fred Jones, Patrick Star, Bart Simpson

Practice using criteria and evidence to narrow brainstormed ideas.

Extension/design-defense group

Scooby-Doo, Velma Dinkley, Daphne Blake, SpongeBob SquarePants, Sandy Cheeks, Lisa Simpson

Analyze tradeoffs and defend redesigns with stronger evidence.
