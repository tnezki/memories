Review Station 1 - Research & Evidence - Answer Key

Question 1

Name one feature of credible engineering evidence.

Answer: It is relevant, observable/measurable, and gathered from a trustworthy source or fair test.

Question 2

Why is an unsupported opinion weak evidence?

Answer: It does not provide verifiable support for the design decision.

Question 3

Give one example of test evidence.

Answer: Any valid measurement from a controlled prototype test.

Question 4

How can research affect a design?

Answer: It can suggest principles, materials, mechanisms, or variables worth testing.

Review Station 2 - Criteria & Constraints - Answer Key

Question 1

A device must work within 30 cm. Criterion or constraint?

Answer: Constraint.

Question 2

A device must run for at least 20 s. Criterion or constraint?

Answer: Criterion.

Question 3

Why must both be checked?

Answer: A design must succeed while staying within limits.

Question 4

Write one possible constraint for a classroom prototype.

Answer: Any valid material/time/size/safety limit.

Review Station 3 - Brainstorm & Compare - Answer Key

Question 1

Why generate more than one idea?

Answer: To compare alternatives instead of accepting the first idea.

Question 2

Name one useful comparison criterion.

Answer: Performance, cost, safety, reliability, ease of construction, etc.

Question 3

What should happen before selecting a final idea?

Answer: Compare ideas against criteria, constraints, research, and test evidence.

Question 4

Why can two ideas both be reasonable?

Answer: Different ideas may satisfy criteria in different ways/tradeoffs.

Review Station 4 - Failure & Redesign - Answer Key

Question 1

A prototype fails at the same joint three times. What should be investigated?

Answer: That joint/connection.

Question 2

Why change one major variable at a time?

Answer: To make cause-and-effect clearer.

Question 3

What should a redesign statement include?

Answer: Failure/evidence, design change, and expected effect.

Question 4

Why repeat tests after redesign?

Answer: To check reliability and confirm the improvement.

Extension Station 5 - Research to Prototype - Answer Key

Question 1

A source suggests higher friction slows motion. Propose a test.

Answer: Change surface friction while holding other major variables constant and measure motion time.

Question 2

What measurement would matter?

Answer: A relevant outcome such as run time/speed.

Question 3

How would you keep the test fair?

Answer: Hold materials/setup/starting conditions constant except the tested variable.

Question 4

What result would support the source-based redesign?

Answer: Consistent measured improvement in the target outcome.

Extension Station 6 - Defend the Best Design - Answer Key

Question 1

What makes a design defense convincing?

Answer: A clear claim supported by relevant evidence and reasoning.

Question 2

Why include tradeoffs?

Answer: The best design may balance competing criteria/constraints.

Question 3

How should failed trials be used?

Answer: As evidence for diagnosing and improving the design.

Question 4

Write a one-sentence CER frame for a redesign.

Answer: Any valid claim-evidence-reasoning frame.
