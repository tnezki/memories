Engineering Design Process - Research, Brainstorming, and Redesign · Stem

Review + Extension / Transfer
Name: ____________________Date: __________
Review
1. Research: cause and effect

A marble reaches the finish in 3.2 s but repeatedly leaves the track at a sharp turn. Name one factor that could affect travel time and one factor that could affect reliability. For each factor, state the effect you would predict before rebuilding.

2. Requirement vs. research

Which note is a research/design factor rather than a requirement?
A. The base must fit inside 12 in x 12 in.
B. A very shallow slope may slow the marble, but if it is too shallow the marble may stop.
C. The structure must be free-standing.

3. Brainstorm for difference

Idea A is a tall zig-zag switchback track. Describe an Idea B that is meaningfully different but still tries to maximize marble travel time. Then name one tradeoff between the two ideas.

4. Compare brainstorms

Two teams propose:
Design A: a long, shallow switchback with side walls.
Design B: a shorter, steeper spiral with no side walls.
If the goal is the longest reliable travel time, choose which one you would prototype first and justify your choice with two engineering factors.

5. Evidence to redesign

Version 1 data: Trial 1 = 8 s, failed at the same sharp turn; Trial 2 = 7 s, failed at that turn; Trial 3 = 6 s, successful. Propose one specific Make It Better change and explain why the test evidence supports it.

6. Strong redesign explanation

Which Make It Better statement gives stronger engineering evidence, and why?
1. 'I will make the track better.'
2. 'Because the marble left the outside of the second turn in two trials, I will add a taller outside wall there so the marble stays on the path.'

Extension / Transfer
7. Transfer: structural redesign

A long-span structure holds the load, but the center bends noticeably while the joints remain secure. Which redesign is most directly supported by the evidence?
A. Add diagonal bracing near the center.
B. Add more tape to every joint.
C. Shorten the required span.
Choose and justify.

8. Evaluate improvement quantitatively

Version 1 best successful marble time is 12 s. After adding side walls, Version 2 has three successful trials and a best time of 15 s. By how many seconds did the best time improve? What is the percent increase from Version 1? Does the evidence support the redesign? Explain.
