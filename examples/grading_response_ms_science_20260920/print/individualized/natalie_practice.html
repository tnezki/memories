Extend your strong design process by making the evidence-to-reasoning link more precise.

1. Cause and effect A tower with a wide base stands for 10 seconds, while a similar tower with a narrow base tips. Explain one scientific reason the wider base can be more stable.

2. Use test data Trial A is 88 cm and stands for 10 seconds. Trial B is 64 cm and falls before 10 seconds. Write one evidence sentence that supports the claim that Trial A was the more successful design.

3. CER reasoning Complete the reasoning: 'A wider base can make a tower more stable because ...' Your explanation should describe how the design resists tipping.

4. Design extension You must still use only 8 straws and 5 paper clips. Describe one change that could increase height without giving up the wide, stable base. Explain the trade-off you would watch for during testing.
