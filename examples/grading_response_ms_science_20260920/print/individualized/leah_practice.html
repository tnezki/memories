Focus on turning observations and data into a clear claim-evidence-reasoning explanation.

1. Evidence from data Trial 1 is 28 cm and does not stand for 10 seconds. Trial 2 is 30 cm and does stand for 10 seconds. Which trial better meets the challenge, and what exact data supports your choice?

2. Reasoning Explain how a low center of gravity can help a tower resist tipping. Use the words 'center of mass' or 'weight' and 'base.'

3. Fix a CER A student writes: Claim: 'My design worked.' Evidence: 'It was sturdy.' Rewrite the evidence so it uses a measurable test result.

4. Fair redesign test You want to test whether a wider base improves stability. Describe what you would change and name two things you would keep the same so the comparison is fair.
