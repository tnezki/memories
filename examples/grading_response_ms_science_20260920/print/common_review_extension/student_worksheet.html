Tallest Tower Engineering Challenge · MS SCIENCE

Review + Extension / Transfer
Name: ____________________Date: __________
Review
1. Criteria and constraints

A tower must be at least 40 cm tall and may use no more than 25 straws. Identify the criterion and the constraint.

2. Fair testing

Two tower designs are being compared. Name two conditions that should stay the same so the test is fair.

3. Evidence

Tower A stands 9 s and Tower B stands 27 s. Which design has stronger evidence of stability?

4. Failure analysis

A tower repeatedly bends at the same joint before falling. What should the team investigate first?

5. Cause and effect

Explain one reason a wider base can make a tall tower more stable.

6. Redesign choice

A tower is stable but too short. Which redesign best targets the problem: widen the base, increase height while preserving bracing, or add weight at the bottom?

Extension
7. Tradeoffs

A redesign is taller but less stable. Explain the tradeoff and propose one next test.

8. Evidence-based redesign

A tower lasts 10 s, then 18 s after adding cross-bracing. What should the team conclude and test next?
