Review Station 1 - Criteria & Constraints - Answer Key

Question 1

A bridge must span 30 cm and use 15 sticks or fewer. Name the criterion and constraint.

Answer: Criterion: span 30 cm; constraint: 15 sticks or fewer.

Question 2

Why can a design meet a criterion but still fail the challenge?

Answer: It may violate a constraint or fail another criterion.

Question 3

Give one example of a material constraint.

Answer: Any valid limited-material example.

Question 4

Give one measurable success criterion for a tower.

Answer: Example: remain standing for 30 s.

Review Station 2 - Fair Tests - Answer Key

Question 1

Why should two tower tests use the same table?

Answer: To keep testing conditions consistent.

Question 2

Name one variable to keep constant when testing base width.

Answer: Examples: height, materials, joint method.

Question 3

Why are repeated trials useful?

Answer: They reduce the chance that one unusual result controls the conclusion.

Question 4

What is wrong with changing base width and tower height at the same time?

Answer: You cannot tell which change caused the result.

Review Station 3 - Read the Evidence - Answer Key

Question 1

A lasts 12 s; B lasts 24 s. Which is more stable in this test?

Answer: B.

Question 2

What number should appear in a convincing comparison?

Answer: The measured test result(s).

Question 3

Is “it looked better” strong engineering evidence? Why not?

Answer: No; it is vague and not measurable.

Question 4

Turn “Design C was best” into an evidence-based statement.

Answer: A valid statement that cites measured data.

Review Station 4 - Target the Failure - Answer Key

Question 1

A joint bends first every trial. What should be redesigned?

Answer: The joint/connection.

Question 2

A stable tower misses the height requirement. What should be targeted?

Answer: Height while preserving stability.

Question 3

Why is random redesign inefficient?

Answer: It may not address the actual failure or unmet criterion.

Question 4

Complete: The evidence shows ____, so we will change ____.

Answer: Any valid evidence-linked response.

Extension Station 5 - Design Tradeoffs - Answer Key

Question 1

A wider base helps stability but uses more material. Name the tradeoff.

Answer: Stability versus material use.

Question 2

A taller tower meets height but tips sooner. What changed positively and negatively?

Answer: Height improved; stability decreased.

Question 3

Why should teams record tradeoffs?

Answer: To make informed decisions among criteria and constraints.

Question 4

Propose a next test for a tall but unstable tower.

Answer: A valid controlled test of bracing/base/support.

Extension Station 6 - Plan the Next Iteration - Answer Key

Question 1

A redesign improves time from 11 s to 20 s. What should happen next?

Answer: Repeat or test the specific change again while controlling other variables.

Question 2

What evidence would convince you a redesign works?

Answer: Consistent measured improvement across fair tests.

Question 3

Why is one successful trial not enough?

Answer: It may be due to chance or uncontrolled conditions.

Question 4

Write a claim-evidence-reasoning statement for a successful redesign.

Answer: Any valid CER using a measured result.
