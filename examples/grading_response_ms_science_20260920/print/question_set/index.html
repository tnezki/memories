Whiteboard Indy

Use engineering evidence to explain testing, failure, and redesign decisions.
← Activity Options
Structure

Individual → independent practice

Setup

Each student gets a whiteboard and one marker.

Directions
  1. Notes are welcome.
  2. Try something before asking for an answer.
  3. Write large and clearly.
  4. Use a partner for reasoning, not copying.
  5. Fix mistakes and discuss what changed.
Goal: Individual accountability + low-stakes problem entry
Materials

Set 1 classroom presentation

Looks Like Success
  • Students explain before comparing final answers.
  • Everyone has a visible thinking role.
  • Disagreements are resolved with evidence.
Doesn't Look Like
  • Copying without explanation.
  • One student doing all the thinking.
  • Moving on with an unresolved disagreement.

Structure

Partner explanation + alternating roles

Setup

Directions
  1. Partner A explains and Partner B records.
  2. Coach with questions, not answers.
  3. Switch roles after each problem.

Quiz-Quiz-Trade

Use engineering evidence to explain testing, failure, and redesign decisions.
← Activity Options
Structure

Pair → quiz → coach/check → trade cards

Setup

Give each student one Cut-Apart Question Card.

Directions
  1. Stand, mix, and pair with the nearest available partner.
  2. Partner A reads the card; Partner B answers and explains.
  3. Partner A coaches or asks for a check before confirming the response.
  4. Switch roles using Partner B's card.
  5. Trade cards, thank your partner, and mix again.
Goal: Retrieval + explanation with repeated partners

Fan-N-Pick

Use engineering evidence to explain testing, failure, and redesign decisions.
← Activity Options
Structure

Four rotating team roles with a card deck

Setup

Teams of four share one stack of Cut-Apart Question Cards.

Directions
  1. Student 1 fans the cards.
  2. Student 2 picks a card and reads the prompt.
  3. Student 3 answers and explains.
  4. Student 4 checks, coaches, or asks for evidence.
  5. Rotate roles clockwise and repeat.
Goal: Equal participation + team coaching

Structure

Mix → pair → respond → switch

Setup

Each student carries one Cut-Apart Question Card.

Directions
  1. Mix around the room until the teacher signals pair.
  2. Partner A reads the card; both partners think before discussing.
  3. Share and compare responses.
  4. Switch to Partner B's card.
  5. Trade cards if directed, then mix again.
Goal: Movement + repeated explanation

Inside-Outside Circle with Cards

Use engineering evidence to explain testing, failure, and redesign decisions.
← Activity Options
Structure

Paired circle discussion with rotation

Setup

Form equal inside and outside circles; each student holds one Cut-Apart Question Card.

Directions
  1. Face the partner across from you.
  2. Use one partner's card as the prompt and both respond.
  3. Compare reasoning and resolve disagreement.
  4. Switch to the other card if time allows.
  5. Outside circle rotates when directed.
Goal: Fast repeated practice with many partners
