Major Strengths

  • The class is generally strong with factoring and algebraic structure.
  • Most students handled line equations, interval work, and core equation solving successfully.
  • Several students showed clear reasoning even when the final answer contained a small arithmetic or completion error.

Top Actionable Patterns

  • A repeated class pattern is stopping before every requested part or representation is complete.
  • Some students factor correctly but then miss a requested sum or product of factors/solutions.
  • Coordinate arithmetic, one-variable arithmetic, and perpendicular-line follow-through need targeted checking for a smaller group.
Highest-leverage class target: After a correct setup, use a final-answer checklist: complete every requested part, perform the requested operation, and verify arithmetic or substitution.

Suggested Instructional Groupings

Extension / Transfer

Daffy Duck; Lola Bunny

Use multi-part transfer problems and require exact forms plus justification.

Precision / Completion

Bugs Bunny; Tweety Bird

Keep the strong core algebra, but focus on the final requested representation, sum/product, or equation form.

Accuracy / Follow-Through

Sylvester Cat; Porky Pig

Use shorter mixed sets with explicit arithmetic and final-answer checks before moving to transfer.
