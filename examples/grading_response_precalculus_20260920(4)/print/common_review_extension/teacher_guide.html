Teacher Guide - Common Worksheet

Pre-calculus - Circuit Training I (Algebra 2 Review)

Review + Extension / Transfer

1. Review - Interval notation

Write \(-2 \le x < 5\) in interval notation.
Answer: \([-2,5)\)
Teacher move: Ask students to connect each inequality symbol to the correct bracket or parenthesis before writing the interval.
Student discourse move: Compare why -2 uses a bracket while 5 uses a parenthesis.

2. Review - Midpoint accuracy

Find the midpoint of \(\left(-\frac{7}{3},\frac12\right)\) and \(\left(\frac53,-\frac32\right)\).
Answer: \(\left(-\frac13,-\frac12\right)\)
Teacher move: Have students average x-coordinates and y-coordinates on separate lines before simplifying.
Student discourse move: Explain which arithmetic step is easiest to make an error on and how you checked it.

3. Review - Solve and verify

Solve \(\frac{x-2}{3}+\frac{x+1}{6}=\frac32\).
Answer: \(x=4\)
Teacher move: Ask for the least common denominator first, then require a substitution check.
Student discourse move: Compare clearing denominators first versus combining fractions first.

4. Review - Sum of factors

Factor \(x^2-7x+12\), then give the sum of the two binomial factors.
Answer: \((x-3)+(x-4)=2x-7\)
Teacher move: Separate the factorization step from the requested operation so students do not stop too early.
Student discourse move: State the factors first, then explain what changes when the prompt asks for their sum.

5. Review - Complete and factor

Choose \(c\) so \(x^2-14x+c\) is a perfect-square trinomial, then factor it completely.
Answer: \(c=49\) and \(x^2-14x+49=(x-7)^2\)
Teacher move: Require both requested outputs: the value of c and the final factored form.
Student discourse move: Explain why half of -14 is used before squaring.

6. Review - Circle features

For \((x+2)^2+(y-4)^2=36\), state the center and radius.
Answer: Center \((-2,4)\); radius \(6\).
Teacher move: Ask students to identify the sign change in the center and the square root used for radius.
Student discourse move: Explain why the center x-coordinate is -2 even though the equation shows +2.

7. Extension / Transfer - Perpendicular line

Find an equation of the line through \((4,-1)\) perpendicular to \(3x-2y=8\).
Answer: \(2x+3y=5\) (equivalently \(y=-\frac23x+\frac53\)).
Teacher move: Have students rewrite the given line in slope-intercept form before taking the negative reciprocal.
Student discourse move: Justify the perpendicular slope and show how your final equation contains the given point.

8. Extension / Transfer - Absolute value product

Solve \(|3x-1|=8\), then give the product of the two solutions.
Answer: Solutions \(3\) and \(-\frac73\); product \(-7\).
Teacher move: Require both branches before allowing students to compute the product.
Student discourse move: Explain why an absolute-value equation creates two linear equations here.
