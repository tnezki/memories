Porky Pig - Individual PracticeName: ____________________

Follow-through practice on coordinate arithmetic, equations, and final-answer checks.

1. Midpoint

Find the midpoint of \((-7,8)\) and \((3,-4)\). Show the x- and y-averages separately.

2. Interval notation

Write \(x<6\) in interval notation. Check that the smaller endpoint appears first.

3. Perpendicular line

Find the line through \((4,1)\) perpendicular to \(y=\frac{3}{2}x-5\).

4. Linear equation

Solve \(7x+9=4x-12\) and verify your solution.

5. Complete the square

Choose \(c\) so \(x^2-10x+c\) is a perfect-square trinomial, then factor it completely.
