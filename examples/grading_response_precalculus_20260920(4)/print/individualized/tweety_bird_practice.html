Tweety Bird - Individual PracticeName: ____________________

Precision practice on final forms, representations, and line equations.

1. Interval notation

Write \(x> -6\) in interval notation.

2. Line equation

Find the line through \((0,9)\) with slope \(-\frac{3}{2}\). Write the complete equation.

3. Sum of factors

Factor \(x^2-8x+15\), then give the sum of the two binomial factors.

4. Circle features

For \((x+1)^2+(y-6)^2=16\), state the center and radius.

5. Perpendicular line

Find the line through \((2,-4)\) perpendicular to \(y=\frac12x+7\).
